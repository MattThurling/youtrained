How it works

  1. You tell us who you are. A name is enough. A channel link lets us check every upload.
  2. We look you up in three public datasets that AI labs use to train and test music models. They're lists of YouTube links, not audio, so nothing of yours is uploaded.
  3. You get a plain answer with the evidence underneath: which dataset, which song or video, and what you can do about it.

Or browse

Artists with the most songs listed · Channels with the most videos listed · Labels the datasets use to describe the music.

What we check against

  • LAION-DISCO-12M (LAION, 2024): 12 million songs on YouTube Music, listed for training general music models.
  • AudioSet (Google, 2017): about a million ten-second YouTube clips labelled as music, used to train and test audio models.
  • MusicCaps (Google, 2023): 5,521 described clips, the standard test for text-to-music models.
